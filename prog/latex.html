Part 1: Definitions

  1. Voltage (V): The electric potential difference between two points.
    Unit: volts (V)

  2. Current (I): The rate of flow of electric charge.
    Unit: amperes (A)

  3. Resistance (R): The opposition to current flow in an electrical circuit.
    Unit: ohms (Ω)

  4. Power (P): The rate at which electrical energy is transferred by an electric circuit.
    Unit: watts (W)

Part 2: Formulas

  • Ohm’s Law: V = I × R

  • Power: $$P = V \times I \quad \text{or} \quad P = I^2 \times R \quad \text{or} \quad P = \frac{V^2}{R}$$

Part 3: Practice Problems

  1. A current of 2 A flows through a 5 Ω resistor. What is the voltage across the resistor?

  2. A device uses 120 V and draws 3 A of current. What is the power consumed?

  3. If a resistor has a resistance of 10 Ω and the power used is 40 W, what is the current?

  4. What is the resistance in a circuit if the voltage is 12 V and the current is 0.5 A?
