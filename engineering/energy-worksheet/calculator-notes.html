Calculator Notes

Looking at the calculator, how do we find kinetic energy if we have the equation:

E_k= \frac{1}{2} mv^2

When m = 0.6 kg and v = 3 \frac{m}{s}

The steps

\frac{1}{2} as a decimal is 0.5, so we put in 0.5.

Next we put in our mass. That’s 0.6.

Next we need to put in v^2. What does the 2 mean?

The 2 means “squared,” which means that number multiplied by itself.

That means 3 times 3.

But we don’t have to type that on a calculator, because we have a button for it. Hit the button that looks like x^2.

When we put that all together, you’re going to press 0.5 x 0.6 x 3 x^2. You should get 2.7, and our units of energy are Joules, or the letter J. So your answer should be 2.7 J.
