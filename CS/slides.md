###  "Inspect Element" Your Way to Internet Fame

<!-- can do? too difficult? -->

This is a classic "hacker" trick that every freshman should know. It doesn’t actually change the website for everyone else, but it changes it on your screen.

* **The Goal:** Make it look like you have 10 million followers on Instagram or that a major news site just wrote an article about you.
* **How to do it:** 1\. Right-click any text on a website in Chrome or Edge. 2\. Click **Inspect**. 3\. Find the text in the code window that opens, double-click it, and type whatever you want.
* **The Fun Part:** Screenshot it and prank a friend. It’s the easiest way to understand that the internet is just a bunch of editable code.
