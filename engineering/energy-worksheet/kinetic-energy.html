Elastic Launch Energy Worksheet

Definitions

Elastic potential energy (stored in a stretched rubber band or spring) is: \begin{equation} E_{\text{elastic}} = \frac{1}{2}kx^2 \end{equation} where:

  • E_{\text{elastic}} = elastic potential energy (J)

  • k = spring constant (N/m)

  • x = stretch distance (m)

Kinetic energy (KE) is the energy of motion: \begin{equation} E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2 \end{equation} where:

  • E_k = kinetic energy (J)

  • m = mass of object (kg)

  • v = velocity (m/s)

At a launch angle of 45^\circ, the horizontal distance traveled is: \begin{equation} D = \frac{v^2}{g} \end{equation} where:

  • D = distance traveled (m)

  • g = acceleration due to gravity (9.8 m/s^2)

Example Problems

Example 1: A rubber band with spring constant k = 20 N/m is stretched 0.15 m. Find the elastic potential energy stored. \begin{align*} E_{\text{elastic}} &= \frac{1}{2}(20)(0.15)^2 \\ &= \frac{1}{2}(20)(0.0225) \\ &= 0.225 \text{ J} \end{align*}

Example 2: A 0.05 kg foam dart leaves the launcher at 6 m/s. Find its kinetic energy. \begin{align*} E_k &= \frac{1}{2}(0.05)(6)^2 \\ &= \frac{1}{2}(0.05)(36) \\ &= 0.9 \text{ J} \end{align*}

Example 3: A dart is launched at 45^\circ with velocity 8 m/s. Find the distance traveled. \begin{align*} D &= \frac{8^2}{9.8} \\ &= \frac{64}{9.8} \\ &\approx 6.53 \text{ m} \end{align*}

Exercises

For a 2: Elastic Energy

  1. A spring with k = 10 N/m is stretched 0.2 m. Calculate E_{\text{elastic}}.

  2. A rubber band launcher has k = 25 N/m and is stretched 0.1 m. How much energy is stored?

  3. A spring with k = 30 N/m is stretched 0.05 m. Find the elastic potential energy.

For a 3: Convert Elastic Energy to KE

Assume no energy loss (E_{\text{elastic}} = E_k)

  1. A 0.1 kg object is launched using a spring stretched 0.2 m with k = 15 N/m. Find its velocity.

  2. A rubber band launcher has k = 40 N/m and stretch 0.1 m. It fires a 0.05 kg projectile. Find v.

  3. A spring with k = 12 N/m is stretched 0.3 m to launch a 0.08 kg puck. Find its takeoff velocity.

  1. A 0.1 kg dart leaves at 5 m/s. How far does it travel?

  2. A projectile launches at 7 m/s. Calculate the distance.

  3. A 0.2 kg ball launches at 10 m/s. How far does it travel?

  4. A foam ball travels 6 m when fired at 45^\circ. Solve for v.

  5. A launcher fires a ball 8 m. Find the speed needed.
