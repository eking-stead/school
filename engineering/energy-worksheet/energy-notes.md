## Kinetic Energy

Energy in movement

 $E_k= \frac{1}{2} mv^2$

 m = mass (kg)

v = velocity (m/s)

## Potential Energy

The kind of potential energy we care about for this project is **elastic potential energy**. This is what builds up when you pull back on a spoon or rubber band.

$E_p = mgh$

m = mass (kilograms)

h = height (meters)

g = gravitational constant ($9.81 \frac{m}{s^2}$)
