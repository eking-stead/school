# Claude Suggestions

## Other 9th-10th options worth considering

* The Outsiders — still a workhorse for a reason, hits identity/class themes hard
* Speak (Anderson) — heavier topic (assault) but extremely teachable, short
* The House on Mango Street — vignette structure is great for teaching voice and works well with reluctant readers
* Fahrenheit 451 — shorter than 1984, pairs naturally with media literacy units
* Long Way Down (Reynolds) — verse novel, fast read, great for engagement and poetic device teaching

## 11th-12th options

* Frankenstein — rich for ethics/sci-fi/Romanticism, pairs well if you want a throughline from Odyssey-style epic to gothic novel
* The Great Gatsby — classic AP-adjacent staple
* Beloved or The Bluest Eye (Morrison) — heavier lift but huge payoff if your students are ready
* Brave New World — pairs interestingly against 1984/Fahrenheit if you did one in 9-10
* Their Eyes Were Watching God — strong voice/dialect study
