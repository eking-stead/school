# Warm up 1

Use variables to write the following constraints:

Admire is keeping track of his protein and carbs

* He wants at least 5 oz of protein each day
* He wants at most 10 oz of carbs each day
* He wants to eat more oz of protein than carbs
* His protein and carbs combined can’t be more than 20 oz
* He wants his oz of protein to be at least twice his oz of carbs


# Warm up 2

Headen is trying to decide how many pigs, cows, and goats to purchase for his farm. Represent the following statements using variables.

* He needs at least 10 cows
* He needs more pigs than cows
* The total number of animals has to be less than 50
* The number of pigs and cows together must be no more than twice the number of goats
