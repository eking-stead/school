AI Ethics Research Project

When is it ethical to use AI?

Project Overview

You will become an expert on one ethical question about AI. By the end of the unit, you will present your findings in a science-fair-style setup.

Final Product:
  • Left: Explain AI like you're talking to a 5-year-old
  • Center: Answer your research question
  • Right: AI art vs human art
Big Question: When does using AI help people — and when does it cause harm?

Day 1 – Question & Curiosity

Focus: What do you want to become an expert on?

  • Explore examples of AI in the real world
  • Choose a research question
  • Begin reading a technical article
Engagement Challenge: Write your question as a debate starter: "Is it okay if AI _____?"

Day 2 – Understanding AI

Focus: How does AI actually work?

  • Finish reading your technical article
  • Take notes in simple language
  • Start researching your question
Explain Like I'm 5: Can you explain AI without using the words algorithm, data, or computer?

Day 3 – Ethics & Evidence

Focus: Is AI helping, harming, or both?

  • Finish your research
  • Identify benefits AND risks
  • Begin building your trifold
Ethics Check: Who benefits the most from this AI? Who might be harmed?

Day 4 – Build & Create

Focus: Turn research into something people want to read.

  • Finish trifold content
  • Create AI vs Human art
  • Check rubric requirements
Design Tip: If someone stands 6 feet away, can they understand your main idea in 5 seconds?

Presentation Day 🎤

Focus: Communicate clearly and confidently.

  • Set up your trifold
  • Practice explaining your work
  • Engage with questions
Audience Goal: Make at least one person rethink how they feel about AI.
