# Necessary pre-skills for the unit

## Finding area of a rectangle

To find the area of a rectangle, multiply its **length** times its **width**.

![Rectangle area](img/rect.png)

## Finding area of a triangle

Finding the area of a triangle is the same as finding the area of a rectangle, then cutting it in two. 

![Triangle area](img/tri.jpg)

## 3/30/26 Building the Pyramid

* The pyramid will have 100 layers.
* The bottom layer will be 100x100 cubes = 10,000 cubes
* The next layer up will be 99x99 cubes = 9,801 cubes
* All the way to the top layer with 1 cube.
* Adding them all up will total 338,350 cubes!
